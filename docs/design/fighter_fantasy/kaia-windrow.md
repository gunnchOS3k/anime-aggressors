# Kaia Windrow — Fighter Fantasy

**Lane:** wind / air / storm / flight / pressure  
**Aura grammar:** controls air

## Fantasy statement

Kaia sells upward lines, ribbon lag, and pressure control. Projectiles / blades should read as **gale ribbons / pressure discs**, translucent and directional—not opaque rectangles.

## Presentation priorities

1. Upward silhouette + glider pack readable  
2. Air control reads before damage numbers  
3. Gale VFX secondary
