# Ember Vale — Fighter Fantasy

**Lane:** fire / heat / combustion  
**Aura grammar:** overwhelms  
**Golden Slice subject:** yes

## Fantasy statement

Ember is a forward-leaning sprinter who sells heat pressure: acceleration bursts, ember gauntlets, flame-tail volume. Projectiles should read as **embers / heat bolts**, not grey rectangles. Attacks overwhelm space rather than poke politely.

## Presentation priorities

1. Silhouette readable at thumb distance  
2. Heat-lane palette accents on sockets / VFX only  
3. Projectile and aura secondary to body
