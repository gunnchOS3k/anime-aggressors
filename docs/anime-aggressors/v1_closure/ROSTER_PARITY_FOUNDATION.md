## Original control foundation, all 18 sources

`tools/authored_animation/blender/aa_support_controls.py` measures actual weighted
Boot bottom vertices, sole/heel/ball pivots and the live forward knee plane.
It provides explicit support replant, pelvis load and foot-roll operations on
the original 22 deform bones, reusing the established offline constraint. No
generic FK-copy rig, added deform bones, stretching, mesh regeneration, imported
animation or action replacement is used. Optional rigid accessory offsets pivot
about Head; they do not fix independent hair weights or runtime object tracks.

`inspect_roster_support.py` opens all 18 preserved `.blend` files and exercises
the controls **in memory**. It checks action/geometry/rest/weight preservation,
loaded heel/ball roll and support targets. Source files are not saved. The first
straight-leg heel-roll failure is retained; the corrected recipe authors hip/knee
compression first, with the same anatomical reach/error tolerance.

The 18 source binaries and shipping GLBs are unchanged. Their persistent control
installation, final independent hair/cloth weighting and full action baking are
still open. At approximately 15 GiB free, no full-roster binary production or
native export campaign is started. Source-space controls and references do not
calibrate the visible boots to the stage floor.

## Bounded runtime category

`data/animation/support_exchanges.json` contains nine original, finite per-fighter
replant scores directed from the nine bibles: committed combustion, planted mass,
compact electrical catch, flowing wind, measured crystal, orbital redirection,
oblique phase, inward absorption and ordered construction. No Kaia/Juno action is
copied to another fighter. These scores are WIP source direction, not final full-
body acting or new ANI05/06 Blender actions on the other seven.

The existing grounded-return support constraint consumes those scores for
locomotion-to-idle and movement-to-attack startup. Support and swing feet exchange
at different source-keyed phases instead of moving both simultaneously. Starting
pose comes from the actual previous gait phase. Startup exchanges retire before
the authoritative active pose; ordinary attack recovery retains its existing
settle deadline. Hurt, jump, shield or renewed movement interrupts the exchange
immediately. Root position, movement inputs and all combat authority remain owned
by the existing simulation.

The original ANI06 failed Kaia stop/Juno light-handoff traces and mixed comparison
remain untouched. New measurements must be reported against those exact recipes;
no stance metric is silently changed to claim success. A maximum ankle motion
alone cannot distinguish an intentional lifted replant from skating.
