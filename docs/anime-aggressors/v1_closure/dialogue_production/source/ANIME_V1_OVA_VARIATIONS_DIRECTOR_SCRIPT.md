# Anime Aggressors — OVA / Campaign Variations Director Script

**Draft.** One continuous Kaia-led OVA and six shorter route variations, not seven unrelated feature episodes. All timings are *editorial proposals* to validate against rendered action, not current game playback evidence.

## Film grammar and emotional structure

- **Kaia OVA:** about 38–52 minutes proposed depending on combat montage density and pace; use one cohesive dramatic arc. Do not compress a grief beat into a ten-second title card.
- **Six Campaign Variations:** about 8–15 minutes each proposed; reuse shared cosmic establishing shots but never duplicate First Loss acting/camera/objective, climax or personal resolution.
- **Convergence:** separate shared event finale, about 20–30 minutes in Story Bible concept, staged around seven full-route completions.
- Gameplay can include repeatable encounters/retries; watch edit preserves narrative meaning without pretending the footage proves playability.
- Original production music only or music with verified licensed use; no franchise samples/themes.

## Six Campaign Variations

Each route supplies its own protagonist POV, six recruit micro-scenes, a unique First Loss dramatic set piece, and a Gray transformation. Use linked `node_id` dialogues, not cloned Kaia subtitles.

### Ember — But now I know when it should light the way instead of clearing it.

**First Loss:** Nix • **Theme:** We built a hearth, not a weapon.

**Variation act/shot plan**

1. **Opening contradiction (0–2m):** `ember-vale:prologue`; close on the flaw in a mundane decision before cosmic danger.
2. **Relationships (2–4m):** six recruit encounters; foreground Nix and show why their disagreement matters.
3. **Accord/breach (4–6m):** shared imagery, cut to Ember observing a consequence Kaia did not see.
4. **First Loss (6–9m):** `ember-vale:first_loss`; use its distinct gameplay objective as cinematic action, not a generic collapse.
5. **Puppet/release/grief (9–11m):** show first authentic gesture on release, changes to inner voice after Essence 1 and 2.
6. **Gray and quiet ending (11–15m):** protagonist integrates the others without ceasing to be themself; Yin and Yang remain unreconciled questions.

**Cinematic centerpiece — script excerpt:**

- **Ember:** “I have always saved time by pushing.”
- **Nix:** “Not this time. Give me room.”
- **Ember:** “There. My hands are still. Why is it still collapsing?”
- **Nix:** “Because the damage was already inside the walls.”
- **Ember:** “Let me take your place.”
- **Nix:** “The interval is yours to protect. My route is already committed.”
- **Ember:** “I heard your warning as a challenge.”
- **Nix:** “Then let it become a lesson.”
- **Ember:** “I will hold this gap. You will not vanish into my fire.”

## Sevenfold Convergence finale

1. **Reunion:** the camera passes to each Gray fighter without centering Kaia as superior. Each voice names a flaw they still have.
2. **Trial:** each player-controlled identity leads one real exchange; responsibility passes rather than accumulating into one chosen hero.
3. **Equilibrium:** Yin and Yang remain separate healthy principles when bounded by consent; fight objective preserves both sides, not a forced kill.
4. **Conversation:** each fighter describes one choice they will continue making.
5. **Unlock:** the competitive Yin/Yang contracts are normalized under fair rules. Do not blur cosmic boss power with Versus frame data.

**Do not grant Story progress from OVA pause/seek/return or watch-only cinematics.**

### Legal and engineering rules

- Choose voices from stock/licensed synthesizers and retain the voice/model license with every delivered audio asset; never imitate a named celebrity or fictional franchise performer without rights.
- Local Mac `say` is appropriate for **private review only** pending clearance for redistribution. Do not ship Apple system-generated recordings merely because they can be produced.
- Keep subtitle text canonical within the draft pack; no synthesized voice is the only record of dialogue.
- Avoid embedding actual temp WAVs in Git unless licensing and asset sizes are checked. Store reproducible voice name/model/version plus line ID and audio hash in a sidecar manifest.
- For speech lengths, generate TTS files per cue ID with padding (~120 ms), loudness normalization and safe subtitle timing. The editor may expand scene duration, but cannot change battle objectives via VO duration.
- When TTS unavailable, show readable subtitles; production audio cannot be silently assumed green.
- Male/female playable presentation preserves same scripted soul. Alternative voice timbres can be selected without rewriting plot or combat authority.
- Text accessibility and hearing-impaired captioning must stand independently of audio.
