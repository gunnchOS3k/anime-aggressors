Existing approved canon: seven First Loss pairings and The Last Vector direction (October 9). All other wording below is proposed, not merged or deployed.

## Reading conventions

- `PRE`: cinematic/setup; `MID`: event-driven bark after a real player action; `POST`: result/aftermath.
- Do not trigger MID/POST by elapsed watch time in playable mode. Bind MID/POST to proper gameplay/receipt signals.
- VO/TTS lines use speaker role; a male/female presentation changes actor/voice selection but not canon diction.
- All seven routes are alternate focal continuities, not seven global deaths.
- Musical score and SFX are placeholders to be newly composed or appropriately licensed.

## Core proposition

Distinct people can coexist without any one identity forcing the rest into silence or uniform perfection. Gray is not an erasure; it is the protagonist understanding six perspectives while remaining themself.
