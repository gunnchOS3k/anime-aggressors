- [ ] Hair or crest is geometry or cards, and it moves

- [ ] Match-camera body height is in the readability band in the model bible
- [ ] Element accent does not replace the body
- [ ] Idle, light, heavy, special, hurt, and victory change the silhouette
- [ ] Side-by-side with the V4.5 board, an outside reviewer agrees it is the same person
- [ ] Puppet or Gray row is flagged story-only
