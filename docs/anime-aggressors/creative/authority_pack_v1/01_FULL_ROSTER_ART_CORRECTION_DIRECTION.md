# Anime Aggressors — Full Roster Art Correction Direction V1
## Canonical creative redirection from runtime placeholders to faithful stylized 3D chibi fighters

**Authority:** Creative art-direction authority  
**Status:** Owner-approval candidate for canonical V1 direction  
**Scope:** Ember, Rook, Juno, Kaia, Nix, Orion, Vesper, Yin, Yang; male/female presentations; Black/White Puppet states; seven Prismatic Gray forms; select screen, match presentation, results, and story presentation.

---

# 1. Decision

The current block/proxy runtime art is not the target.

It may remain temporarily as:
- technical scaffolding;
- rig/loading tests;
- collision and pipeline validation;
- regression fixtures.

It may **not** be treated as:
- final roster art;
- G6 visual-readability completion;
- G9 final-art completion;
- evidence that male/female presentation is finished.

## Visual North Star

> **Faithful stylized 3D chibi adaptations of the V4.5 character sheets.**

The translation to chibi must simplify proportions without simplifying identity.

The target is:
- premium stylized 3D;
- cel-shaded;
- anime-human facial structure;
- materially elemental bodies rather than ordinary human-skin completion;
- oversized readable head;
- compact action-ready body;
- expressive hands and feet;
- strong fighter-specific silhouette;
- V4.5 palette and iconography;
- readable on handheld screens;
- strong enough for character select, combat, victory, and story scenes.

Reject:
- box mannequins;
- Minecraft-like forms;
- generic skeleton reskins;
- generic low-poly humanoids recolored per fighter;
- almost-identical male/female meshes;
- VFX used to hide weak modeling;
- base-roster puppet masks.

---

# 2. Source-derived continuity rules

The following are locked:

1. Base spectrum fighters have recognizable human facial structure with elemental material completion.
2. Male/female are presentations of the same soul, personality, moveset, animation authority, hitbox authority, and competitive identity.
3. Base roster does not use Black/White Puppet masks.
4. Black Puppet is Yin-controlled; White Puppet is Yang-controlled.
5. Every spectrum fighter requires NORMAL, BLACK PUPPET, WHITE PUPPET, and Prismatic Gray story presentation capability.
6. Essence progression is 0 → 1 → 2 → 4 → 6; at 6 the Anchor reaches Prismatic Gray/Chromatic.
7. Prismatic Gray means all seven colors held in equilibrium, not colorlessness.
8. Every fighter must still read without VFX.

---

# 3. Chibi translation law

**PRODUCTION DECISION V1**

The chibi adaptation should feel like the V4.5 cast compressed into a stronger game-readable proportion system, not redesigned into toys.

## Shared silhouette rules

All fighters should have:
- head large enough for readable hair/face at roster-card and handheld scale;
- torso compact enough to preserve chibi appeal;
- hands enlarged enough for attack posing;
- feet large enough to make stance and landing readable;
- limbs long enough to preserve martial posing;
- face planes simple enough to survive cel shading;
- 3–7 major costume/material masses visible at a glance.

The roster must be identifiable:
- as a black silhouette;
- at 25% render scale;
- with VFX disabled;
- in grayscale;
- when duplicated in a mirror match.

## Chibi must not erase archetype

Rook must remain visibly heavier than Juno.
Juno must remain visibly faster/narrower than Rook.
Kaia must remain aerodynamic.
Nix must remain structured.
Orion must remain orbital.
Vesper must remain unstable/asymmetric.
Ember must remain forward/aggressive.
Yin and Yang must remain cosmic principles rather than recolored humans.

---

# 4. Male / female presentation doctrine

Same:
- soul;
- personality;
- archetype;
- move IDs;
- frame data;
- damage;
- collision/hitbox authority;
- action IDs;
- root-motion assumptions;
- gameplay reach envelope;
- power structures;
- animation library.

Different enough to be obvious at character select:
- face shape;
- eye framing;
- hair silhouette;
- torso/hip/shoulder presentation within gameplay envelope;
- garment/armor cut;
- secondary silhouette;
- selected accent details.

Never:
- “male + scaled-down female”;
- “female = faster-looking gameplay body” when the archetype is heavy;
- sexually exaggerated proportions that fight the established chibi/action readability;
- different competitive reach.

The model switch is only complete when the player can identify the selected presentation without reading the Male/Female UI label.

# 5. Full roster art direction

## 5.1 Ember Vale — The Living Furnace

**Core read:** controlled combustion, martial pressure, relentless forward intent.

Preserve:
- red identity `#EF2B1F`;
- furnace core;
- ignition gauntlets;
- charred ceramic/obsidian material;
- thermal seams;
- wedge/forward-diagonal silhouette;
- heat crown/vent motifs.

Chibi translation:
- compact fighter torso with a forward lean;
- large expressive gauntlets;
- flame/hair mass swept backward to reinforce velocity;
- face readable and confident, never hidden by excessive flame;
- lower legs/feet built for hard plants and catch steps.

Do not:
- turn Ember into a generic “fire mage”;
- use floating fire disconnected from body mechanics;
- make both body presentations share the same head/hair mesh with trivial scaling.

Select pose:
- one foot forward, gauntlets on centerline, core already alive.

Story states:
- Black Puppet: fire starves inward; one red pulse remains.
- White Puppet: fire becomes perfectly repeated geometry; one dark imperfection remains.
- Gray: other Essences travel through combustion, but Ember remains fire-first.

Acceptance question:
> Can the player feel pressure and ignition before Ember attacks?

## 5.2 Rook Ironside — The Walking Bastion

**Core read:** a fortress that decided to move.

Preserve:
- orange `#F47A20`;
- stone/earth/forged-plate material;
- tectonic seams;
- broad low center of gravity;
- layered armor;
- heavy circular joints;
- column/block language.

Chibi translation:
- broadest torso of the spectrum roster;
- head remains large/chibi but nested into heavy shoulder mass;
- large forearms/hands;
- compact legs with oversized planted feet;
- armor plates overlap rather than becoming literal rectangular boxes.

Do not:
- confuse “block shape language” with crude cube modeling;
- make female Rook read as a speed fighter;
- remove weight for cuteness.

Select pose:
- planted stance; armor settled; one hand ready to brace.

Story states:
- Black Puppet: armor swallowed inward into quiet void gaps; one orange seam remains.
- White Puppet: over-perfect symmetric white lattice.
- Gray: spectrum materializes as armor states while mass remains the governing read.

Acceptance question:
> Does Rook feel heavy before any attack begins?

# 6. Puppet-state art law

Puppet states are not simple recolors.

## Black Puppet / Yin control

Narrative meaning:
- individuality is being subtracted;
- information is disappearing;
- timing and gesture are reduced.

Visual:
- black-dominant material;
- masked presentation permitted here;
- fighter's original hue survives as one small truthful signal;
- original silhouette remains identifiable but partially collapsed.

## White Puppet / Yang control

Narrative meaning:
- identity is being over-defined;
- every detail is retained but forced into imposed order;
- perfect repetition suppresses autonomy.

Visual:
- white-dominant material;
- masked presentation permitted here;
- unnatural symmetry;
- original hue survives as constrained detail;
- animation timing becomes over-exact.

The same soul must still be inferable beneath control.

# 7. Prismatic Gray art law

Prismatic Gray is not monochrome emptiness.

Base:
- graphite;
- silver;
- smoke-gray;
- neutral black/white values.

Accents:
- all seven ROYGBIV colors;
- shown contextually during movement and power use.

Rule:
> The fighter's original identity remains the carrier medium.

Examples:
- Ember carries the spectrum through combustion.
- Rook through mass/material.
- Juno through current/acceleration.
- Kaia through flow/wind.
- Nix through constructs.
- Orion through orbital nodes.
- Vesper through phase echoes.

Story Prismatic versions may be mechanically enhanced.
Competitive Prismatic versions retain competitive parity.

# 8. Select-screen and showcase correction

Normal player-facing select must prioritize:
- attractive card art;
- fighter name;
- archetype;
- clear male/female presentation control;
- large faithful live preview;
- concise identity text.

Technical art-review telemetry belongs behind dev/review mode.

The large preview must be:
- close enough to inspect face/hair/material;
- framed consistently;
- lit to preserve cel shading;
- rotated or posed without clipping;
- visibly responsive to presentation switching.

A model is not approved if the card is attractive but the live 3D preview is not.

---

# 9. Match presentation correction

At gameplay camera distance:
- every fighter must remain identifiable by silhouette;
- faces need not remain detailed, but head/hair/costume masses must;
- hit poses must read without VFX;
- fighter scale must not be so small that the chibi model becomes a colored dot;
- stage/HUD cannot dominate the characters.

Use the source readability tests:
- silhouette;
- anticipation/contact/recovery;
- VFX-off;
- 25%-scale;
- grayscale;
- duplicate-fighter;
- freeze-frame contact.

---

# 10. Asset retirement policy

Classify current assets:

`KEEP_TECHNICAL_ONLY`
- block/proxy meshes;
- generic mannequins;
- temporary collision bodies.

`REFERENCE_ONLY`
- current Vesper skeleton-like proxy if useful for chibi proportion exploration.

`REPLACE_PLAYER_FACING`
- any block Kaia/Yin/Yang currently presented to players;
- any generic chibi body that materially contradicts fighter identity.

`PRESERVE_IF_FAITHFUL`
- existing assets that already satisfy V4.5 identity, rig compatibility, and human visual review.

No destructive deletion until replacements are validated.
