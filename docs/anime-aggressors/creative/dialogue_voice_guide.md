Doctrine. These are not recorded lines and they are not in the game. Writers may change wording. They may not change the soul.

Rules: one breath, no wiki talk, same diction for male and female, puppet lines belong to Yin or Yang with one leftover word from the soul.

| Soul | Player-facing voice | Example of the temperature, not a locked script | Puppet leftover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ember | Hot, short, forward | She talks like the next step is already on fire. | “burn” |
| Rook | Few words, heavy | He finishes sentences as if setting a stone down. | “hold” |
| Juno | Fast, then a stop | She arrives in the middle of her own thought. | “sooner” |
| Kaia | Calm, room-making | She asks before she pushes. At Gray she still asks. | “flow” |
| Nix | Precise, almost kind | She describes a plan the way other people describe weather. | “lattice” |
| Orion | Slow, certain | He names where something will land. | “orbit” |
| Vesper | Late, amused, a half-truth | She answers the question you were about to ask. | “late” |
| Yin | Soft, finished | Offers rest. Never shouts. | the whole line is Yin |
| Yang | Clear, finished | Offers the completed shape. Never rambles. | the whole line is Yang |

Black Puppet: Yin’s quiet, plus the leftover word, then silence.
White Puppet: Yang’s clarity, plus the leftover word, repeated once.

Do not write a monologue that explains Prismatic Gray. Show the ribbons.
