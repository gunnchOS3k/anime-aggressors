# Shared agent quality contract

This additive guidance applies to Codex and Cursor; preserve stronger existing owner and scoped instructions.

- Approved character sheets, fighter bibles and source-authority receipts are canonical. Preserve anatomy, silhouette and individual identities across all nine fighters and both bodies; no golden-slice substitution.
- Codex leads character design, Blender/rig/source animation, forms/Prism/Puppet/True Aspect, nine ultimate cinematics, materials/VFX, character sound, camera/game feel and final aesthetic recommendations. Cursor may independently own tests, CI, controllers, PartyLink/network protocols, instrumentation, saves/replays, diagnostics, accessibility and defect reproduction/release reconciliation.
- Cursor must preserve Codex-produced art/rig/animation/cinematic/material/VFX contracts. Record art defects with source-bound evidence and an isolated proposal for Codex review; no silent replacement. Do not copy one fighter's acting as another's final production.
- Do not share a writable branch or worktree between simultaneously active agents. Cursor uses a separate task branch/checkout after inspecting existing worktrees; never switch/reset this active production checkout. Integrate reviewed commits serially after both agents checkpoint and release the write lease. Do not automatically merge or cherry-pick unreviewed art candidates.
- Original, owned/licensed sources only; no copied third-party game/anime performances/assets. Generic mannequins/recolors/WIP geometry are not final art. Art claims require editable source, importable export and provenance.
- Gameplay authority comes from resolved runtime events. Preserve timing, collision/contact ownership, balance defaults, modes, save compatibility and earned progression unless a specific change is explicitly authorized. Never seed acceptance or weaken tests to turn them green.
- Preserve before/after evidence for visual changes and retain negative findings. Distinguish source validation, engine import, actual native gameplay, device measurements and human approval. CI is not artistic acceptance.
- Preserve worktrees, owner saves, original assets/actions and evidence. No cleanup/deletion without separate approval. Internal free space must remain at least 18 GiB AFTER anticipated allocations; remeasure before each build/export/capture. External archive is verified immutable storage, not an active build disk.
- No merge, tag, release, deployment or publication authorization. All pending human art/gameplay/balance/mix/device/release gates stay pending/false. Existing scoped owner approvals are not broadened.
