## Implemented pipeline, awaiting original-art execution

- Export the two selected original armatures, visible weighted meshes and frozen
  source-stage anchors. Blender4.0 `ACTIVE_ACTIONS` merges only those two actions;
  `export_anim_single_armature=false` avoids exporting one actor's old library
  onto another. One named clip preserves frames0–24 at60Hz.
- Record every original bone's evaluated source-world position at all25frames.
  Save/reopen, original geometry/weights/rest/material/action hashes and the
  offline placement contract remain required. No hitbox, frame data, damage,
  gameplay root or body scale is changed.
- Inspect the actual binary for two owned skeletal skins, original joint IDs,
  real weights, both actors' nonconstant joint channels, a single synchronized
  clock and embedded dependencies. Reject static-only or one-actor substitutes,
  changed clocks and moving non-joint actor roots.
- Import through Godot `GLTFDocument`, drive its actual `AnimationPlayer` and
  compare source joint positions/anchor transforms at all25frames. The50µm bound
  is the existing float/FK serialization bound, not an anatomical/art tolerance.
  No method/audio/gameplay callback tracks execute.
- Native results must match the exact export SHA and cannot be synthetic fixtures
  for local production. Export/import success cannot enable the remaining batch.

[4.0 exporter source](https://github.com/blender/blender-addons/blob/blender-v4.0-release/io_scene_gltf2/__init__.py).
